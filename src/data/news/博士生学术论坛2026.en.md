On September 22, 2026, the State Key Laboratory of Chemical Resource Engineering held its 2026 Doctoral Academic Forum at the East Campus of Beijing University of Chemical Technology (BUCT). Ph.D. students Ling Chen and Wenzhe Song from our group participated in the forum and received second and third prizes, respectively.

The forum featured two venues, with 32 doctoral students giving oral presentations and 115 presenting posters on recent research advances. Professors, early-career faculty members, and graduate students from three research divisions joined the discussions. Following applications, preliminary reviews by the laboratory, oral and poster presentations, and evaluation by the judges, the forum awarded 10 first prizes, 22 second prizes, 55 third prizes, and 60 excellence awards.

Ling Chen delivered an oral presentation titled “Construction and Applications of DNA-Encoded Nanovaccine Adjuvants.” Her research and responses to questions earned her a second prize. Wenzhe Song received a third prize for the poster presentation. Both students discussed their work with faculty members and fellow students, gaining valuable feedback and further developing their academic communication skills.
