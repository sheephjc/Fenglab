The 15th National Conference on Analytical Chemistry of the Chinese Chemical Society took place at the Changsha International Conference Center in Hunan from September 18 to 22, 2026. Jointly organized by the Chinese Chemical Society's Committee on Analytical Chemistry and Hunan University, the conference focused on “Analytical Chemistry: Precise Sensing, Intelligent Drivers, and Cross-Disciplinary Integration.” Experts and scholars from across China gathered to discuss advances at the forefront of analytical chemistry and interdisciplinary collaboration.

Professor Feng Li attended the conference by invitation and delivered an invited talk at the Young Scientists Forum. Her presentation highlighted the group's latest research findings and significant progress in inhaled nucleic acid therapeutics.
