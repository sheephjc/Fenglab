On September 19, 2026, Lanzhou University held a conference celebrating the 80th anniversary of its chemistry discipline and advancing its high-quality development at the Student Activity Center Auditorium in the western section of the Chengguan Campus. Professor Feng Li, the group's principal investigator, returned by invitation as an alumni representative, joining alumni from China and abroad, faculty, students, and distinguished guests to mark this milestone.

![Lanzhou University's conference celebrating the 80th anniversary of its chemistry discipline and advancing high-quality development](/images/news/lanzhou-chemistry-80th-anniversary/anniversary-conference.png)

*Lanzhou University's conference celebrating 80 years of chemistry and advancing high-quality development*

Under the theme “Advancing Knowledge and Practice, Serving the Nation through Chemistry,” the conference welcomed prominent scholars from China and abroad. Attendees included academicians Kuiling Ding, President of Shanghai Jiao Tong University; Wenping Hu, President of Xiamen University; Jin Zhang, Executive Vice President of Peking University; and He Tian, Vice President of the Chinese Chemical Society, as well as Professor Omar M. Yaghi, a recipient of the 2025 Nobel Prize in Chemistry. The conference reviewed the development of chemistry at Lanzhou University since its founding in 1946, highlighting its roots in western China and dedication to education, and explored future directions for high-quality development.

During the event, Professor Feng Li held in-depth exchanges with former teachers, classmates, and college leaders. She expressed her gratitude for the education she received at her alma mater and her warm wishes for the continued development of its chemistry discipline.
