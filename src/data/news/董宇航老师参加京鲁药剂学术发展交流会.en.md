The 2026 Beijing–Shandong Pharmaceutics Academic Exchange was held on September 18–19, 2026. Yuhang Dong was the sole attendee from Feng-lab@Buct.

![Group photo at the 2026 Beijing–Shandong Pharmaceutics Academic Exchange](/images/news/jinglu-pharmaceutics-2026/group-photo.jpg)

*Group photo at the academic exchange*

The meeting explored advances in pharmaceutics and drug development. Presentations covered computational pharmaceutics for 3D-printed timed-release formulations, applications of quantitative pharmacology in drug discovery, and uses of artificial intelligence in personalized treatment and pharmaceutical research. Dong learned about recent work in these areas and exchanged ideas with other participants. On the afternoon of September 19, he also joined a visit to a pharmaceutical company to learn about industry practice in drug development.
