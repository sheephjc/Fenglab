Vaccines are essential tools for preventing and controlling infectious diseases, and their development is advancing from traditional formulations toward a new generation of precise and intelligent vaccine systems. Although widely used subunit vaccines offer high safety and clearly defined components, their lack of effective innate immune stimulation often results in insufficient cellular immune activation and limited immune-memory persistence. These constraints remain particularly important for respiratory viral infections, complex viruses, and diseases that require strong T-cell-mediated protection.

![JACS article title and author information](/images/news/新型纳米疫苗佐剂工程/jacs-article-header.png)

*The research article was published in the Journal of the American Chemical Society (JACS)*

In collaboration with a research institute of China National Biotec Group, the team developed a new RSV subunit vaccine platform based on an orthogonal nanoadjuvant design. The system uses Mn/Al layered double hydroxide (Mn/Al-LDH) nanomaterials as an immunoregulatory carrier and integrates DNA structures containing CpG sequences to activate distinct immune pathways synergistically. The aluminum component primarily promotes antibody-mediated humoral immunity, while manganese ions and CpG activate the cGAS-STING and Toll-like receptor (TLR) pathways, respectively, creating a multidimensional immune activation pattern resembling a “high-entropy-like immunostimulatory effect.” The nanovaccine markedly activated antigen-presenting cells, enhanced CD4⁺ and CD8⁺ T-cell responses, and established long-term immune memory. In an RSV infection animal model, the strategy achieved stronger viral clearance and better lung-tissue protection than a conventional aluminum-adjuvanted vaccine, providing a new technical route for developing next-generation RSV vaccines and vaccines against other viruses. The findings were published this year in the *Journal of the American Chemical Society*.

Looking ahead, the team will continue to address major infectious-disease prevention and control needs by advancing novel nanoscale delivery systems, composite immunoadjuvants, and precision vaccine design. The team aims to accelerate the translation of fundamental research into innovative vaccine products and applications, contributing to independent innovation in China’s biopharmaceutical sector and the advancement of vaccine technologies.
