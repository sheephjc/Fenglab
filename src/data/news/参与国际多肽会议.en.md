From July 5 to 7, 2026, the 19th Chinese International Peptide Symposium and the 16th International Peptide Symposium (CPS2026) were held at Wyndham Beijing North. Members of Feng Lab attended the meeting and exchanged ideas with researchers from universities, research institutes, and industry around the world.

![Feng Lab members at CPS2026](/images/news/参与国际多肽会议/group-photo.webp)

*Feng Lab members at CPS2026*

Under the theme “Fusion Innovation, Peptides Inspire a Better Life,” the symposium highlighted advances in peptides and chemical biology, peptide therapeutics, peptide materials, and health through plenary lectures, focused sessions, and poster presentations.

![Professor Feng Li taking part in an academic discussion at CPS2026](/images/news/参与国际多肽会议/li-feng-presentation.webp)

*Professor Feng Li taking part in an academic discussion at the symposium*

During the meeting, Professor Feng Li joined academic discussions with fellow researchers on peptide materials and their biomedical applications.

![Xueyu Fan presenting a research poster](/images/news/参与国际多肽会议/xueyu-fan-poster.webp)

*Xueyu Fan presenting the group’s research during the poster session*

Xueyu Fan shared recent research from the group in a poster presentation and discussed the work with other participants. The symposium broadened the group’s academic perspective and offered new ideas for future research and collaboration.
