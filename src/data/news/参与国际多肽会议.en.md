From July 5 to 7, 2026, the 19th Chinese International Peptide Symposium and the 16th International Peptide Symposium (CPS2026) were held at Wyndham Beijing North. The meeting brought together leading experts and scholars from around the world in peptide science and biomedicine to discuss frontier advances and future directions. Professor Feng Li, Yuhang Dong, Wenzhe Song, Xueyu Fan, and Jianyu Gao attended the symposium and engaged in in-depth academic exchange with colleagues from China and abroad.

![Feng-lab@Buct members at CPS2026](/images/news/参与国际多肽会议/group-photo.webp)

*Feng-lab@Buct members at CPS2026 in Beijing*

During the symposium, Professor Feng Li delivered an invited talk titled “Nucleic Acid Drug Delivery and Pulmonary Fibrosis Therapy Based on Biomacromolecular Phase Separation.” The talk focused on innovative applications of biomacromolecular phase separation in nucleic acid drug delivery and presented the group’s latest progress in pulmonary fibrosis therapy.

![Professor Feng Li delivering an invited talk at CPS2026](/images/news/参与国际多肽会议/li-feng-presentation.webp)

*Professor Feng Li delivering an invited talk on biomacromolecular phase separation, nucleic acid drug delivery, and pulmonary fibrosis therapy*

Xueyu Fan also presented the group’s latest research in a poster session. During the presentation, she discussed the work face to face with experts from China and abroad, answered questions in detail, and gathered valuable suggestions that will help refine the research.

![Xueyu Fan presenting a research poster](/images/news/参与国际多肽会议/xueyu-fan-poster.webp)

*Xueyu Fan presenting the group’s latest research during the poster session*

Participation in CPS2026 strengthened the group’s academic presence at the intersection of peptide science and biomaterials and provided valuable opportunities for direct exchange with leading international researchers. Feng-lab@Buct will continue to advance research in biomacromolecular phase separation and nucleic acid drug delivery, with the goal of developing more innovations with clinical translation potential.
